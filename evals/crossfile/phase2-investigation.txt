
Investigation procedure:
1. Identify the calls and predicates whose behavior determines the answer.
2. Search for their definitions, then READ their implementations. A name,
   declaration, search hit, or familiarity with an API is not proof of behavior.
3. Follow relevant calls across files until the security-sensitive operation
   or effective guard is established. Check conditional compilation against
   the stated build assumption. If search results are truncated, narrow them.
4. Do not repeat the supplied function instead of inspecting missing context.
5. Reserve your last model call for the final answer. If the investigation
   remains incomplete, say insufficient_evidence rather than assuming safety.
